# PyLab Mobile App Privacy Policy

**English** | [Русский](README.ru.md)

**Published and effective as of:** October 9, 2026

## 1. General Provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes what information is processed when using the **PyLab** mobile application (the "App") for Apple devices (iPhone, iPad), distributed through the App Store.

1.2. The App is owned and developed by **Arseniy Manakov**, an individual (the "Developer"). The Developer is not a legal entity or a registered sole proprietor and distributes the App in his own name.

1.3. Any person who has installed the App on their device is considered a user (the "User").

1.4. This Policy has been prepared in accordance with Federal Law of the Russian Federation No. 152-FZ of July 27, 2006 "On Personal Data" and Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.

1.5. By installing and using the App, the User confirms that they have read this Policy. If the User does not agree with the Policy, they should stop using the App and delete it from their device.

## 2. Information Collection

2.1. **The Developer does not collect, receive, store, or process Users' personal data**, or any other information about Users and their devices.

2.2. In particular, the App:

- does not require registration, account creation, or sign-in;
- does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, or other contact details;
- does not determine the device's location;
- does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track the User's activity across other apps and websites;
- contains no advertising and no third-party analytics, statistics, advertising, or tracking services;
- does not transmit User-created content (code, projects, files, settings) over the network;
- does not sell information or share it with third parties.

2.3. The Python interpreter is built into the App and runs directly on the User's device. No internet connection is required to run programs, and the User's code is not sent to any external servers.

## 3. Information Stored on the Device

3.1. For the App to function, the following is stored locally on the User's device:

- projects, files, and source code created or opened by the User;
- App settings (for example, the theme and editor preferences);
- progress in the "Learning" section.

3.2. This information is stored only in the User's device storage. The Developer has no access to it.

3.3. The User may delete this information at any time using the App's features or by deleting the App from the device. When the App is deleted, all of its local data is removed by the operating system. If iCloud Backup is enabled on the device, the App's local data may be included in the device backup. Such backups are performed by Apple in accordance with its own privacy policy; the Developer has no access to them.

## 4. Access to Device Features

4.1. The App may request access to the following device features only in response to a User action:

| Feature | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Photo Library | Selecting images for use in Python projects |
| Files | Opening, importing, and saving files and projects via the system file picker |

4.2. Images and files selected by the User are processed only on the device and are not transmitted anywhere.

4.3. The User may revoke granted permissions at any time in the device's Settings. In that case, the related App features may become unavailable, while all other features will continue to work.

## 5. Disclosure of Information to Third Parties

5.1. The Developer does not disclose information about Users to third parties, as it does not collect such information.

5.2. If the User chooses to send a file or code from the App via the system Share menu (for example, to a messenger, email, or cloud storage), the transfer is initiated by the User and performed by the operating system. Any further processing of the information is carried out by the service chosen by the User in accordance with its own terms.

5.3. Apple may collect information related to downloading the App from the App Store and to the device's operation (including diagnostic reports, if the User has allowed sharing them with app developers in the device settings). Such processing is carried out by Apple in accordance with the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The Developer may receive from Apple only aggregated, anonymized statistics that do not allow the User to be identified.

## 6. Contacting the Developer

6.1. If the User contacts the Developer by email on their own initiative, the Developer receives the User's email address and the content of the message.

6.2. This information is used solely to respond to the inquiry, is not shared with third parties, and is deleted at the User's request.

## 7. Children

7.1. The App does not collect information about any Users, including children under the age of 13 (or another age set by the laws of the User's country). The App may be used for educational purposes, including by children under the supervision of parents or legal guardians.

## 8. Security

8.1. Since the User's information never leaves their device, it is protected by the security mechanisms of iOS / iPadOS (app data sandboxing, device storage encryption). Users are advised to set a device passcode and keep the operating system up to date.

## 9. Changes to the Policy

9.1. The Developer may amend this Policy. A new version takes effect upon its publication at this address, unless otherwise stated in it. The effective date of the current version is shown at the beginning of this document.

9.2. If the App begins collecting any information, the Developer will reflect this in advance in the Policy and in the "App Privacy" section of the App's App Store page and, where necessary, will request the User's consent.

9.3. The revision history of this Policy is available in the commit history of this repository.

## 10. Contact

10.1. For any questions regarding this Policy or the App, the User may contact the Developer by email: **[pylaboratory@mail.ru](mailto:pylaboratory@mail.ru)**.
